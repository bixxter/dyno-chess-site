Questions.

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

Because it ships Stockfish, and Stockfish is licensed under the GPLv3, which is widely held to be incompatible with the App Store's terms. Downloading it here is the honest option rather than a worse one: the build is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so Gatekeeper is satisfied, and it updates itself directly.

Does it need an internet connection?

No, and it cannot use one. The app ships without the network entitlement at all, so this is verifiable rather than a promise — run codesign -d --entitlements :- "Dyno Chess.app" and look for yourself. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing breaks. You get 14 days of Pro when you first launch it, and after that the app quietly becomes the free version. The board, the island and all nine opponents keep working forever. You will never find your chess game held hostage.

How many Macs does one licence cover?

Three. The licence is a signed file checked on your Mac, so activating does not require the internet either.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days, no reason required. Email debixxter@gmail.com and it gets refunded in full — you don't have to explain yourself. The refund policy spells out the details.

Who am I buying from, and how do I get help?

Dyno Chess is made and sold by Asatulla Dadabaev, a sole trader in Kazakhstan — one person, not a company. Support, refunds and lost licence keys all go to debixxter@gmail.com, and it's read by a person. The privacy policy and terms of sale are on one page.

Which engine is it, and is the source available?

Stockfish, running as a separate process over UCI, exactly as its licence intends. The full GPLv3 text ships inside the disk image alongside a link to the exact source revision the bundled binary was built from.

I don't have a notch. Does it still work?

Yes. On a Mac without a physical notch the island draws itself as a pill at the top of whichever display you choose.
