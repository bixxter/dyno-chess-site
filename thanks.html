Activating it

Don't have the app yet? Download it for Mac first — the key activates a copy already on the Mac, and it will wait as long as you need.

  1. Open Dyno Chess and press ⌘, for Settings.
  2. Go to Licence and paste the key — one long line starting with DYNO1-.
  3. Click Activate. Pro turns on immediately.

The key is checked on your Mac by signature alone — the app makes no network connection to verify it, and it keeps working offline forever. Paste the same key on your other Macs.

If you use Claude Code, the other place worth visiting is Settings → Agents: that is where the session strip is switched on and where Pro removes the daily limit on approving from the notch.
